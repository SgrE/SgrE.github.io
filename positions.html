Research fit

My research focuses on the mathematical foundations of machine learning and the mechanisms of large language models, with an emphasis on learning efficiency and nonlinear approximation. Please read the research themes and publications before getting in touch.

Motivated undergraduates with a strong mathematical foundation, programming skills, or an active interest in research are also welcome to contact me.

Getting in touch

Email your CV and transcript, together with a short explanation of your research interests and why you would like to work with me. You are welcome to write in English or Chinese.

Contact: fanghui.liu@sjtu.edu.cn or fanghui.liu@outlook.com.

Because of the number of enquiries, I may not be able to reply to every email. I will follow up when there is a close match with my research.

Supervision context

I am based at SJTU's Institute of Natural Sciences, with a joint appointment in the School of Mathematical Sciences. I serve as a PhD supervisor in the School of Mathematical Sciences (070100 Mathematics) and the School of Artificial Intelligence (140500). These general supervision affiliations do not imply an opening in every school or year; the Fall 2027 restrictions are stated above.

I am not accepting new students at Warwick. Prospective applicants interested in theoretical computer science, mathematics, or statistics there are welcome to contact my former colleagues; I am also happy to provide introductions and recommendations where appropriate.
