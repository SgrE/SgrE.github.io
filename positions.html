  • 招生方向： 数学科学学院（070100 数学），面向数学与应用数学方向，当前无法招收统计方向。2027 年秋季入学博士生暂不从人工智能学院招收。

Fall 2027 PhD Recruitment

One PhD position is available through the School of Mathematical Sciences.

  • Research area: Mathematics and Applied Mathematics, through the School of Mathematical Sciences (070100 Mathematics); no Statistics places are currently available. I am not recruiting Fall 2027 PhD students through the School of Artificial Intelligence.

  • Direct-entry PhD applicants: Undergraduates who have received the School's summer-camp Outstanding Camper distinction are welcome to contact me.   • Application–assessment route: Applicants via this route (e.g., those who already hold a master's degree) cannot currently be considered. Please wait for subsequent announcements from the School; there is no need to email about this route at present. Changes will be posted on this page.

I have no plans to recruit postdoctoral researchers.
